# Capstone Evaluation Rubric

## Overview

One capstone, two rounds. Everyone is graded on both.

| Round | What is graded | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Round 1 — everyone** | Pitch package + presentation to teaching staff + keep/change decision | **100 pts** |
| **Round 2 — everyone** | Consulting package + working MVP (scored separately) | **100 pts** |

Instructors may combine or report the two scores per cohort policy. Changing industry or use case after the Round 1 presentation does not cost points.

---

## Round 1 Rubric (100 points)

| Dimension | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Research, use cases, opportunities/risks | 20 |
| 2. Dashboard (communication layer) | 25 |
| 3. POC + LangSmith monitoring sample | 25 |
| 4. Cost/timeline + documentation quality | 15 |
| 5. Presentation, feedback use, decision write-up | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

### 1. Research & use cases (20)

- **Exceeds (17–20):** Sector/size context is specific; opportunities/risks are realistic; use cases fit constraints; sources cited.
- **Meets (12–16):** Clear research and plausible use cases; some depth missing.
- **Below (0–11):** Generic or weakly justified; little evidence of research.

### 2. Dashboard (25)

- **Exceeds (21–25):** Stakeholder-ready; metrics justified; communication layer respected; docs clear.
- **Meets (14–20):** Functional and relevant; some design or rationale gaps.
- **Below (0–13):** Hard to read, misaligned metrics, or missing docs.

### 3. POC + monitoring sample (25)

- **Exceeds (21–25):** POC runs and matches a use case; monitoring evidence shows transparency thinking; limits stated honestly.
- **Meets (14–20):** POC and monitoring present and basically work.
- **Below (0–13):** Broken, missing, or unrelated to the pitch.

### 4. Cost/timeline + docs (15)

- **Exceeds (13–15):** Assumptions explicit; numbers grounded; README/repo navigable.
- **Meets (9–12):** Estimates and docs present with minor gaps.
- **Below (0–8):** Hand-wavy numbers or chaotic submission.

### 5. Presentation + decision (15)

- **Exceeds (13–15):** Clear pitch to teaching staff; `round1_decision.md` is specific, including any industry or use case change.
- **Meets (9–12):** Presented; decision recorded with basic reflection.
- **Below (0–8):** Weak/missing presentation or missing decision file.

**Round 1 key questions**

- Would Chleo understand what the AI does?
- Is transparency addressed somehow (dashboard story, monitoring, plain language)?
- Did the student make a real decision after presenting to teaching staff (including changing industry or use case when that was the right call)?

## Round 2 Rubric (100 points) — Everyone

| Dimension | Weight | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Use case definition & POC | 15% | 15 |
| 2. Working MVP | 15% | 15 |
| 3. ROI & risk assessment | 20% | 20 |
| 4. EU AI Act compliance | 20% | 20 |
| 5. GDPR documentation | 10% | 10 |
| 6. Strategic deployment plan (incl. pilot) | 10% | 10 |
| 7. Final presentation | 10% | 10 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100** |

### 1. Use case & POC (15)

- Clear problem, stakeholders, success criteria, scope limits; POC demos the AI capability; documentation reproducible; Round 1 → Round 2 evolution explained, including a new industry or use case if that is what the staff presentation led to.

### 2. Working MVP (15)

- Runs; core AI capability actually works; basic error handling; `mvp_documentation.md` lets someone else start it. A small MVP that runs scores above an ambitious one that does not.

### 3. ROI & risk (20)

- Upfront/ongoing costs, value, 12/36-month ROI, assumptions, break-even; ≥6 risks with likelihood, impact, mitigation across regulatory/technical/ethical/operational.

### 4. EU AI Act (20)

- Correct-enough classification with reasoning; obligations addressed for the class; conformity summary; technical documentation outline.

### 5. GDPR (10)

- Data flows; legal bases; DPIA for highest-risk processing; rights; third-party transfers.

### 6. Strategic plan (10)

- POC → **pilot** → full deployment with milestones; GTM; stakeholder comms; KPIs; commercialisation model. Pilot success criteria are explicit.

### 7. Presentation (10)

- Speaks to business, legal, and technical concerns; MVP demo works or backup recording used; honest about uncertainty.

**Round 2 key questions**

- Is this something a client could say yes/no to?
- Are compliance claims honest (including "minimal risk, and here is why")?
- Does the plan use a pilot before full rollout?

---

## Grading Notes for Instructors

- Round 2 and the MVP are required for everyone. There is no stop-after-Round-1 path.
- Changing industry or use case after the teaching staff presentation is allowed and positive. Do not mark students down for switching.
- If a student's Round 2 uses a different industry or use case than Round 1, they must document it in `round1_decision.md` / the use case doc. That is the expected path after a change, not a problem.
- Keep Campus identifiers stable: Round 1 → `project-5`; Round 2 → `final-project`.
