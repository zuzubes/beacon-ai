# Our trend hierarchy

Looking at new, emerging trends is exciting, but to give structure and focus, you need a multi-layered trend framework. This is how we will build ours at Beacon AI:

## Macro-Trends

These are long-term, macro changes that play out across many years or even decades with a large-scale impact. They summarize all major forces across society, technology, economy, ecology, and politics that impact consumer behavior.

## Mega-Trends

Our mega-trends are the building blocks of the consumer arena. They identify the points of tension that macro-trends create when they intersect with consumers' and businesses' basic needs.

## Sub-Trends

We non-stop identify sub-trends that have arisen from the aforementioned tension. They incite action by highlighting how a brand can start acting on emerging expectations today.
