Sustainability and Alternative Protein Adoption
The food and beverage sector in the US is undergoing a long-term shift toward sustainable sourcing and alternative proteins such as plant-based, cultured meat, and fermentation-derived ingredients. This shift is driven by environmental concerns, supply chain disruptions, and consumer demand for healthier and ethical options.

Executive Summary

The Food & Beverages sector in the U.S. is undergoing transformative shifts fueled by sustainability, technology integration, and personalized wellness demands.
