# Top 20 Trending Products in China — 2026 Report

**Date:** September 1, 2026
**Scope:** China domestic consumer demand signals — trending categories and products across Douyin (抖音), Taobao/Tmall, JD, Xiaohongshu (小红书) and macro consumer reports
**Method:** Cross-validation of independent Chinese sources: 知萌咨询《2026中国消费趋势报告》(Dec 2025), iiMedia Research《2026年中国新消费趋势白皮书》(Jan 2026), 沥金×有米有数《2026年抖音电商的29个品类机会》(Feb 2026), 千瓜数据《2026「活跃用户」研究报告·小红书》(May 2026), 有赞《2026年三大消费趋势洞察》(Feb 2026), 炼丹炉《2026年618大促复盘》(Jul 2026), 星图数据《2026年电商发展报告》(May 2026). Every data point is labeled with source and date. Future projections are marked as 预测值.

> ⚠️ Macro context first: China's e-commerce has entered a stock-market phase — online retail grew only +3.7% in 2025 (Source: 星图数据, May 2026), April 2026 retail sales +0.2% YoY (Source: Morgan Stanley via 新浪财经, Jun 2026), and 618 2026 GMV reached ¥934B, +4.0% YoY (Source: 炼丹炉, Jul 2026). **Total demand is flat; structural winners are growing 15–40%+.** The 20 picks below are all structural winners with data backing, not one-off viral items.

## TL;DR — What matters most

1. **Emotional economy is the biggest new engine** — ¥2.72T track in 2026, from ¥2.31T in 2024 (Source: iiMedia Research, Jan 2026). IP merchandise ("谷子经济") hit ¥202.1B in 2025, +19.7%; 80.3% of consumers engage in emotional consumption at least monthly (Source: 知萌咨询, Dec 2025).
2. **Health is being "precision-tuned"** — 60.9% of consumers increased health spending last year; 78.6% now set quantified health goals (Source: 知萌咨询, Dec 2025). Winners are targeted-effect supplements (sleep/eye/immunity), snack-ified wellness, and body-care upgrades.
3. **Smart & AI hardware is the fastest-scaling sector** — smart home ¥815.4B (+29.0%), service robots ¥185B (+41.8%), AI smart glasses entering mass adoption (Source: iiMedia Research, Jan 2026).
4. **Pet and outdoor "atmosphere" spending keeps compounding** — pet economy ¥811.4B (+15.7%) with buyers prioritizing quality/safety over price; camping "atmosphere gear" (lights, tables, coffee sets) beats pro gear (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026).
5. **For cross-border sellers: several of these are China-first trends now exporting globally** — IP merch, smart-home security, robot vacuums, high-end pet products, "nourishing" base makeup, functional beverages. Domestic winners validate demand you can source and sell overseas.

## Per-Product Detail & Notes

### 1. IP merchandise / 谷子经济 (+19.7%)
Guzi (IP-derived merch: 吧唧 badges, acrylic stands, blind boxes, "pain bags") reached ¥202.1B in 2025, +19.7% YoY, as IP symbols convert virtual fandom into physical consumption for Gen Z (Source: iiMedia Research, Jan 2026). Emotional economy overall: ¥2.31T (2024) → ¥4.62T forecast by 2029. Reference case: 河南博物院考古盲盒 sold ¥46M+ (Source: 知萌咨询, Dec 2025). **Note:** IP licensing is mandatory — this is a licensed-goods category with strict IP compliance.

### 2. Smart pet products (+15.7% sector)
Pet economy ¥811.4B in 2025, +15.7% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). Buyers rank quality (48.2%), safety (47.9%), durability (47.8%) above price (36.6%) — high-end smart feeding, pet strollers, grooming tech have clear headroom (Source: iiMedia). Xiaohongshu: pet life "converges" with owner life — ritual, experience, outdoor/social scenes (Source: 千瓜, May 2026).

### 3. Smart home (+29.0%)
Smart home ¥815.4B, +29.0% YoY, heading to ¥1.8T by 2030 (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). Demand concentrates on basic control + safety monitoring (locks, cameras, lighting) — "simplify life, secure the home" — rather than complex entertainment features (Source: iiMedia). Direct export crossover: US demand for doorbell cameras (+78% YoY, GoDaddy Mar 2026) validates the same category globally.

### 4. Robot vacuums / floor washers (+41.8% service robots)
Service robots ¥185B, +41.8% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026); robot vacuums ¥15.7B (+7.5%) are the mature core. 618 2026: home appliances led all categories at ¥114.6B GMV (Source: 炼丹炉, Jul 2026). Mature domestic brands now exporting aggressively — strong sourcing reference for overseas sellers.

### 5. AI smart glasses
"AI scene power" is a top-10 trend (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025); AI native terminals are launching en masse (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). Purchase drivers: battery life 61.3%, comfort 56.0% — experience-first, brand-last (Source: iiMedia). Rokid-style AR/translation glasses showed the wearable-AI use case (Source: 知萌). Note: electronics certification (SRRC/CCC domestically; FCC/UL for export) is mandatory.

### 6. Mood fragrance (香氛/香薰)
Ritual consumption is mainstream: 79.2% agree daily life needs rituals; 87.1% will pay for them (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025); bedside diffusers and car scents are the entry points of the emotional-economy track (¥2.72T, Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). Fragrance is also one of the few categories with repeat purchase and gifting cycles.

### 7. Precision supplements (+6.2% sector)
Supplements ¥377.5B, +6.2% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). The Douyin rule in 2026: **precision beats vague wellness** — specific-effect products (sleep, eye care, immunity, beauty) outperform general tonics; small size, low unit price wins new buyers (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). 诺特兰德 Omega-3 search volume +300% in 2025 (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025). Compliance: 保健食品蓝帽子 (blue hat) approval for domestic; for export, FDA DSHEA etc.

### 8. Sleep shorts / liquid sanitary pads (+39% price, 2x sales)
Female care upgraded: category price +39%, sales more than doubled (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Drivers: comfort, safety, convenience — sleep shorts and liquid pads are the new mainstream; reusable/new-material formats are the next wave (Source: 沥金).

### 9. Localized skincare (T-zone, lip, neck)
Precise localized care is beating all-in-one products: T-zone care, lip care growing fastest; pore serums and lip serums outperform generalist SKUs (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Skincare demand extends from face to full body (Source: 千瓜, May 2026) — body serums, neck creams, hand care are all in play. "功效 > 颜色": skincare outgrows color cosmetics in downturns (Source: 沥金).

### 10. Men's makeup (concealer, brow)
Men's beauty is still early-stage white space — low-priced trial products (concealer, brow pencils) are pulling in first-time male buyers; profits come later via professional men's lines (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026).

### 11. Snack-ified wellness (蜂蜜水、口袋养生)
"Wellness as a pocket snack" is how to reach young consumers — no brewing, no rituals; ready-to-drink honey water, collagen shots, office tea drinks (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). 蜂解 honey water: #1 category seller, drafted industry standard GH/T1451-2024 (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025).

### 12. Specialty coffee (挂耳、精品速溶、咖啡豆)
Coffee is trading up: pour-over bags, specialty instant, and whole beans are eating into classic 3-in-1 instant share as consumers get "coffee-literate" (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Export signal: US instant coffee sales +1,339% YoY (Source: Shopify via Skailama, Apr 2026) — same upgrade wave globally.

### 13. Camping "atmosphere gear" (+16.1% core)
Camping core market ¥248.3B, +16.1%; driven market ¥1.44T, +24.7% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). On Douyin the rule is: **sell atmosphere, not specs** — lights, tables, chairs, coffee sets for weekend suburban camping outperform pro hiking gear (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Xiaohongshu adds "micro-sweat / light exercise near home" (Source: 千瓜, May 2026).

### 14. Ice & snow gear (+34.8%)
Fastest-growing outdoor sub-segment: ¥84.7B in 2025, +34.8%, forecast ¥194.7B by 2030 (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026). Ski apparel, warm gear, and accessories benefit from sustained winter-sports policy push.

### 15. Home healing furniture & ambiance
Home consumption is shifting from function to self-pleasure — items that create relaxation, ritual, or hobby scenes command premiums (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026); 顾家 zero-gravity "energy cabin" chair is the reference case (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025); Xiaohongshu framing: "适我化" design and corner "微丰容" (Source: 千瓜, May 2026). Mood lights, massage chairs, aromatherapy appliances all fit.

### 16. DIY renovation tools (+ male pro-tool upgrade)
Rental economy drives a self-renovation boom — simple, fast-results tools are a blue ocean (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Men's "tool worship" (reliable, multi-function, textured) extends to home tools and high-end outdoor tools (Source: 沥金). Strong export angle: US home-improvement demand is similarly resilient.

### 17. Kitchen small appliances
Visually demo-able kitchen small appliances are the easiest Douyin hit machines — air fryers, blenders, coffee machines — but need content-iteration capability or tech moats to stay hot (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). 618: appliances led all categories (¥114.6B) (Source: 炼丹炉, Jul 2026).

### 18. Content-creation gear
Creator "arms race": cameras, plus pro-grade lighting, mics, and gimbals, as video becomes the universal social language (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Driven by both creators and ordinary users investing in output quality.

### 19. Kids' shoes
Kids' shoes are rising in price and volume — worn hard, replaced often, easy to differentiate functionally (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Outperforming kids' apparel; parents pay for function-led design.

### 20. Function-specific cleaners
Consumers are abandoning "one bottle for everything": dedicated kitchen degreasers, bathroom mildew removers, and other single-problem cleaners win on clearly visible results (Source: 沥金, Feb 2026). Premium tissues (cotton-soft, moisturizing) show the same specialization trend (Source: 沥金).

| Mood fragrance / ritual consumption | US candle warmers +102% at 98K/mo (Ahrefs, 2026) | Same flame-free comfort demand |
| Snack-ified functional food | US protein soda +667%, functional bev +27.3% (Ahrefs/EMarketer, 2026) | Same convenience-health logic |

Source, certify (FCC/UL/CPSC for electronics; FDA for cosmetics/supplements), and list while overseas demand is still ramping.
