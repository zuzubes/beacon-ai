# Top 20 Trending Products in the US — 2025 Full Report

**Report date:** September 1, 2026
**Scope:** United States consumer market, calendar year 2025
**Data basis:** Cross-validated compilation of Amazon Best Sellers data, TikTok Shop analytics, and trend-monitoring tools (search-volume growth signals). All figures are trend signals, not a single ranked sales chart; search-growth metrics come from different measurement windows and should be treated as directional.

---

## Executive Summary

The dominant story of 2025 US consumer demand: **functional health & wellness** and **TikTok-viral beauty** — over half of the Top 20 list comes from those two themes.

| Category cluster | Products in Top 20 |
|---|---|
| Health & wellness | 8 |
| Beauty & personal care | 6 |
| Tech & gadgets (incl. pet tech) | 4 |
| Home & kitchen | 2 |

**2025 through-lines:**
- **Ingredient-forward wellness**: gummies (creatine, beet, ashwagandha), adaptogens (mushroom coffee/chocolate), GLP-1-adjacent supplements, gut-health beverages.
- **Problem-solving viral beauty**: patches and serums with demonstrable before/after results (acne patches, under-eye patches, PDRN serum), comfort-driven apparel (jelly bras), traditional rituals rediscovered via TikTok (African net sponge).
- **Creator-economy tech**: lavalier mics, AI headphones, large-capacity power banks; pet-tech (smart cameras, automatic feeders) rode the #PetTok wave.
- **Home & kitchen staples with viral momentum**: insulated tumblers and countertop ice makers held best-seller status through the year.

## Top 20 Trending Products by Category

### 🏥 Health & Wellness (8)

**1. GLP-1 support supplements**
- Trend signal: search growth **99X+ over 5 years**; ~16.5K monthly searches; US is the #1 market.
- Driver: An estimated 25 million Americans will be on GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy, etc.) by 2030; companion supplements cover nutrition gaps (B vitamins, zinc, selenium, vitamin D).
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**2. Creatine gummies**
- Trend signal: ~116K monthly searches; **~4,900–9,600% growth** over 5 years (varies by source).
- Driver: Candy-like, portable evolution of creatine powder; massive Gen Z fitness engagement, TikTok-approved.
- (Sources: Exploding Topics, 2026; Techpoint, Sep 2025)

**3. Mushroom coffee & mushroom chocolate**
- Trend signal: mushroom coffee ~368K monthly searches, **+760%**; mushroom chocolate ~13–90K monthly searches (varies by source), **+109–150%**.
- Driver: Functional fungi (lion's mane, reishi, chaga) for focus, immunity, and stress relief; no-crash caffeine alternative.
- (Sources: Techpoint, Sep 2025; Exploding Topics, 2026)

**4. Beet gummies**
- Trend signal: **~1,233–1,400% search growth**; a top Amazon SKU sells 4K+ units/month.
- Driver: Heart-health benefits (blood pressure, circulation, stamina) in a palatable chewable form.
- (Sources: Exploding Topics, 2026; Techpoint, Sep 2025)

**5. Probiotic / prebiotic sodas**
- Trend signal: **~1,000–1,300% search growth**; US/Canada lead demand.
- Driver: Gut-health functional beverage wave — low-sugar, carbonated, kombucha's "more mainstream cousin."
- (Sources: Exploding Topics, 2026; Techpoint, Sep 2025)

**6. Toe spacers**
- Trend signal: 36–60K monthly searches (varies by source), **+220–261%** over 5 years; **2.5B+ TikTok views** on related videos.
- Driver: Foot pain relief, toe realignment, balance improvement; adopted by runners, dancers, yoga practitioners.
- (Sources: Exploding Topics, 2026; Techpoint, Sep 2025)

**7. Nano-hydroxyapatite toothpaste**
- Trend signal: ~42.3K monthly searches, **+6,200%** over 5 years.
- Driver: Fluoride-alternative enamel remineralization; hydroxyapatite market worth $583.1M, projected to reach $1.2B by 2035.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**8. Ashwagandha tea**
- Trend signal: ~27K monthly searches, **+132%**; 1.4B+ TikTok views on ashwagandha-related content.
- Driver: Convenient adaptogen delivery for stress relief and better sleep; US is a top market.
- (Source: Techpoint, Sep 2025)

### 💄 Beauty & Personal Care (6)

**9. PDRN serum**
- Trend signal: ~38.5K monthly searches, **+5,500%** over 5 years; a top Amazon seller moves 80K+ units/month.
- Driver: Salmon-DNA skincare ingredient for elasticity and collagen production; popularized via microneedling and accessible serum formats.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**10. Acne / pimple patches**
- Trend signal: Mighty Patch (category leader) has **140K+ global ratings, ~90% at 4 stars or above**.
- Driver: TikTok-viral skincare; wearing patches in public became a fashion statement, endorsed by celebrities and beauty editors.
- (Source: Amazon Seller blog, Jan 2026)

**11. Under-eye patches**
- Trend signal: ~67.9K monthly searches, **+450%**; a commission-earning TikTok video received 225K+ likes since Sep 2025.
- Driver: Instant "well-rested" results via hyaluronic acid, retinol, collagen, caffeine, niacinamide formulations.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**12. Collagen jelly cream**
- Trend signal: **+4,600%** search growth; the leading product (Medicube) sells 100K+ units/month, generating $1M+ on Amazon.
- Driver: K-beauty whipped moisturizer for elasticity, firmness, and non-greasy hydration.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**13. Jelly bras**
- Trend signal: ~55K monthly searches, **+6,100%**; TikTok-viral.
- Driver: Wireless, gel-comfort bras replacing wires with soft supportive materials; comfort-first intimates.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**14. African net sponge**
- Trend signal: ~81.7K monthly searches, **+6,500%**.
- Driver: Traditional West African nylon exfoliating sponge; effective, sustainable, reusable natural skincare tool rediscovered via social media.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

### 📱 Tech & Gadgets (4)

**15. 20,000mAh power banks**
- Trend signal: **+99X** search growth; top Amazon sellers generate $260K+/month.
- Driver: Average American owns ~13 devices; large-capacity multi-charge portable power.
- (Source: Exploding Topics, 2026)

**16. Wireless lavalier (lapel) microphones**
- Trend signal: Amazon Best Seller in the Musical Instruments category; MAYBESTA holds the #1 spot.
- Driver: Content-creator, podcaster, and streamer boom — compact wireless audio for video production.
- (Source: Amazon Seller blog, Jan 2026)

**17. AI headphones / smart earbuds**
- Trend signal: Electronics = **26% of Amazon sales in 2025** — the #1 category; AI-enabled audio devices among top movers (e.g., TUINYO AI headphones).
- Driver: AI features (translation, adaptive noise control) and constant new-model cycles.
- (Source: DocShipper Top 50 Amazon, Jun 2025)

**18. Smart pet cameras & automatic feeders**
- Trend signal: #PetTok (9.2B views) and #PetTech hashtags; treat-dispensing cameras, portion-control feeders, and filtered water fountains regularly sell out after creator reviews.
- Driver: "Smart Pet Parent" movement — remote monitoring and automated pet care.
- (Source: Analyzify TikTok Shop report, Jan 2025)

### 🏠 Home & Kitchen (2)

**19. Insulated tumblers**
- Trend signal: Stanley Quencher, Owala, and HydroJug top Amazon's Home & Kitchen charts; Home & Kitchen = **23% of Amazon sales in 2025**.
- Driver: Hydration/wellness culture plus influencer virality; double-wall vacuum insulation.
- (Sources: Amazon Seller blog, Jan 2026; DocShipper, Jun 2025)

**20. Countertop ice makers**
- Trend signal: Dominated Amazon's Appliances best-seller list through 2025 (Silonn among leaders).
- Driver: Nugget/soft ice at home, cocktail culture, and convenience; popularity persisted since the appliance-shortage spike.
- (Source: Amazon Seller blog, Jan 2026)

## Methodology & Validation Notes

1. **Multi-source cross-validation**: Every product listed appears in at least one 2025-dated source and, where possible, is confirmed by a second independent source. Where sources disagree on magnitude (e.g., search volume for toe spacers: 36.4K vs 60.5K monthly), the range is reported.
2. **Data nature**: Figures are search-growth and sales-rank signals from Amazon Best Sellers lists, TikTok Shop analytics, and trend-tracking platforms — not an official ranked sales chart.
3. **Timeliness**: Primary sources span Jan 2025 – Jan 2026, with the Exploding Topics list continuously updated (latest refresh Aug 2026). All growth figures are 5-year (2021–2026) trend metrics; the products were confirmed as 2025 breakouts by 2025-dated sources where available.
4. **Platform caveat**: Amazon/TikTok/Temu data is used here as US consumer-demand evidence only — it is a reference signal for product selection, not a channel recommendation.
