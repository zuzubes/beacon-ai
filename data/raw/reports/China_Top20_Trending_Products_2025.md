## TL;DR — What defined 2025

1. **Emotional / IP consumption exploded** — 谷子经济 (IP merchandise) exceeded ¥200B in 2025 (Source: QuestMobile, Oct 2025); LABUBU turned Pop Mart into a HK$300B+ market-cap company and a global super-IP (Source: 中新网, Jun 2025); 《哪吒2》merchandise was "一吒难求" (Source: 中新网, Jun 2025).
2. **"Technology going downmarket" created new hardware winners** — action/pocket cameras +252% in unit sales (Source: Sandalwood via 亿欧, Dec 2025), consumer 3D printers priced ~60% lower than 3 years ago (Source: CCTV via 亿欧, Dec 2025).
3. **Neo-Chinese everything (华流当道)** — 国潮 economy forecast ¥2.5T for 2025 (Source: 中研普华 via 亿欧); neo-Chinese wellness drinks and tea-beer (新中式茶啤) were breakout beverage formats.
4. **Health + smart living compounded** — smart home +29.0%, ice & snow gear +34.8%, pet economy +15.7% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026 — 2025 actuals).
5. **For cross-border sellers:** China's 2025 winners are already exporting — LABUBU went global, China action cameras and 3D printers lead internationally, and the neo-Chinese aesthetic (driven by Ne Zha 2) is reshaping overseas demand. These are validated demand signals, not experiments.

## Per-Product Detail

### 1. IP merchandise / Guzi economy (谷子经济)
The year IP fandom became a consumer force: 谷子经济 broke ¥200B in 2025 (Source: QuestMobile, Oct 2025; iiMedia: ¥202.1B, +19.7%). LABUBU made Pop Mart a global phenomenon — Q1 2025 revenue +165–170%, market cap over HK$300B (Source: BBC中文, Jun 2025; 中新网, Jun 2025); JPMorgan called LABUBU "the next Hello Kitty" (Source: 中新网). Badges (吧唧), acrylic stands, blind boxes and "pain bags" drove the emotional economy. **IP licensing compliance is mandatory** — unlicensed production is the #1 legal risk.

### 2. Ne Zha 2 (哪吒2) derivatives
The film entered the global top-5 all-time box office and became Asia's biggest movie ever (Source: 中新网, Jun 2025); its merchandise sold out across toy stores ("一吒难求") and turbo-charged the IP-goods market. A case study in how a domestic cultural hit converts into physical product demand.

### 3. 痛金 (IP emotional spending)
Taobao's #1 annual product: buying IP-branded / collectible items (limited sneakers, hand figures, themed gold jewelry) as an emotional outlet — "I deserve this after a hard day" (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Combines fandom identity with a preservation-of-value angle (IP gold jewelry).

### 4. 拼豆 (fuse-bead craft kits)
#2 on Taobao's list — low-cost craft kits (~tens of RMB) offering "controllable achievement" and stress relief in an uncertain world (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Office/desk hobby market, strong repeat purchase on new patterns.

### 5. Neo-Chinese wellness drinks (新中式养生饮)
#3: herb tea bags, 草本冲剂, 药食同源 ready-to-drink beverages at ¥4–8/bottle — "self-care in a bottle" for busy workers (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Matches 知萌's 健康调频 trend: quantified, portable, ritual health (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025).

### 6. "苏超" football merch
#4: Jiangsu Super League support towels, co-branded apparel, limited badges — sports fandom as identity and social currency, the same energy that made "村超" and "苏超" national phenomena (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025).

### 7. Bag charms (包挂)
#5: cartoon plush, tags, mini-bags hung on bags — the "silent self-introduction" accessory, expressing fandom, hobbies, and pet ownership without words (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). High SKU variety, easy sourcing, strong giftability.

### 8. Ribbed down jackets (排骨羽绒服)
#6: slim-fit ribbed down jackets solved the "warm but bulky" problem (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Tmall Double-11 apparel: Uniqlo, Bosideng, 蕉内 top-3; 雪中飞 jumped to #5 (Source: 天猫双11, Nov 2025). Functional+stylish outerwear remains the winning formula.

### 9. Car mattresses (车载床垫)
#7: the car-as-mobile-home product for camping and road trips — a lifestyle shift toward "go anywhere, sleep anywhere" (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Backed by camping economy growth (core market +16.1%, Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026).

### 10. Thin-soled shoes (薄底鞋)
#8: after years of chunky sneakers, thin-soled shoes won on grounded comfort and natural feel (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025) — a fashion-cycle reversal with strong export carryover.

### 11. AI companion plush (AI陪伴玩偶)
#9: conversational, learning, emotion-responsive plush for solo young adults and empty-nest seniors (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). China's 2025 AI boom (DeepSeek, Sora2, AI music) made AI-native consumer hardware a mainstream expectation (Source: 亿欧, Dec 2025).

### 12. Flash-sale / instant retail (闪购×万物)
#10: not a physical product but the year's defining channel — limited-time flash sales and instant retail delivering "click, pay, own" certainty (Source: 淘宝, Dec 2025). Taobao Flash Purchase + Cainiao/Tmall investment made instant retail a headline battleground (Source: 新榜, Dec 2025).

### 13. Action & pocket cameras (运动相机/口袋相机)
"Technology going downmarket" star: 4.76M action cameras sold by Oct 2025, +252% units, +316% revenue; e-commerce GMV +6,191% (Source: Sandalwood & 《2025中国电商发展报》via 亿欧, Dec 2025). First-person shooting for "出片" culture; entry price ¥300–800, DJI Pocket 3 <¥3,000. Chinese brands (DJI, Insta360) now lead globally — direct export relevance.

### 14. Consumer 3D printers
Consumer 3D printer prices fell ~60% in three years (Source: CCTV via 亿欧, Dec 2025), with free model libraries removing the learning barrier. China's Bambu Lab-style brands dominate the global consumer market — one of the clearest China-tech-export success stories of 2025.

### 15. Neo-Chinese tea beer (新中式茶啤)
Tea + craft beer: 金星啤酒's Chinese-style craft beer sold 100,000 tons in 10 months with 60% repurchase and 2B+ Douyin topic views (Source: 亿欧/惊蛰, Dec 2025). Neo-Chinese flavor innovation applied to the alcohol shelf — a 2025 breakout format.

### 16. Lightweight shell jackets & outdoor (冲锋衣)
Decathlon's lightweight shell jacket sales +67% (Source: 亿欧, Dec 2025); Tmall Double-11 sports: FILA, Nike, Adidas top-3, Lululemon #4 (Source: 天猫双11, Nov 2025). Outdoor apparel moved from "gear" to "daily wear" — the 内行主义/心价比 effect.

### 17. Smart pet products (智能猫砂盆等)
Pet economy ¥811.4B in 2025, +15.7% (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026); smart litter boxes, pet birthday cakes, and pet photo shoots became mainstream emotional spending (Source: 亿欧, Dec 2025). Buyers prioritize quality/safety over price — premium smart pet devices have clear headroom.

### 18. Smart home (智能门锁、安防、照明)
¥815.4B, +29.0% in 2025 (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026) — the fastest large-scale consumer category, concentrated in basic control and home-security functions. 以旧换新/国补 policy added a tailwind (Source: 抖音电商发展数据报告 context, Jan 2026).

### 19. Ice & snow gear (冰雪装备)
¥84.7B, +34.8% in 2025 (Source: iiMedia, Jan 2026) — the fastest-growing outdoor sub-segment, powered by winter sports policy and the Harbin winter-tourism wave.

### 20. Intangible-heritage cultural products (非遗文创、簪花)
"非遗手工" searches +790%, "非遗体验" +387% over Spring Festival (Source: 美团 via 亿欧, Dec 2025); Quanzhou hair-flower (簪花) tourism drew 4.26M visitors and ¥860M in revenue Jan–Jul (Source: 知萌, Dec 2025). Museum/creative cultural products (文创, blind-box archaeology kits) were a top growth pocket (Source: 知萌).
