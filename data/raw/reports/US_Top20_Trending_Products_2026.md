## Per-Product Detail & Sourcing Notes

### 1. Embroidered apparel (+7,043% YoY)
Personalization is the driver — monogrammed/embroidered hoodies and caps are the premium tier of print-on-demand apparel. Search for "embroidered apparel" grew 7,043% YoY (Source: GoDaddy, Mar 2026); embroidered hoodies specifically +120% over 5 years (Source: Printful, Jun 2026). **Seller action:** offer custom embroidery/initial options — this is a differentiation play against mass-printed basics; embroidery machines and apparel blanks are easily sourced, MOQ-friendly, and the customization premium protects margins.

### 2. Exosome serum (+6,400% YoY)
Premium anti-aging skincare using exosome technology (cell-regeneration marketing angle). Search growth +6,400% YoY, retail $50–$300+ (Source: GoDaddy, Mar 2026). **Seller action:** premium positioning, clinical-looking packaging; compliance is the gatekeeper — cosmetics with therapeutic claims fall under FDA drug jurisdiction (see Compliance section).

### 3. Electric scalp massager (+3,710% YoY)
Handheld self-care device, $5–$25 retail, +3,710% YoY search (Source: GoDaddy, Mar 2026); also a TikTok Shop top seller (Source: CJdropshipping, Jun 2026). Low price point = easy entry; water-resistant versions widen appeal. **Seller action:** bundle with shampoo/conditioner sets to raise AOV; avoid hair-growth claims.

### 4. Eyebrow lamination kits (+3,500% YoY)
At-home salon treatment kits, +3,500% YoY search (Source: GoDaddy, Mar 2026). **Seller action:** strong demo-video product; include multi-step kits (glue, serum, tools) and clear shade/usage instructions.

### 5. Beef tallow moisturizer (+2,600%)
"Back-to-basics" natural skincare — searches grew 2,600% between 2023 and 2025 (Source: Shopify data via Skailama, Apr 2026). TikTok-beauty-driven demand, high repeat purchase. **Seller action:** clean-label storytelling; pair with other tallow products (balms, soaps). Verify FDA cosmetic ingredient & labeling rules.

### 6. Niacinamide body lotion (+2,100% YoY)
Body-care extension of the niacinamide trend, +2,100% YoY (Source: GoDaddy, Mar 2026). **Seller action:** body-care premiumization is under-served vs. face serums — position as "facial-grade ingredients for the body."

## Compliance Quick Notes (US market)

| Product group | Key US requirements to verify before listing |
|---|---|
| Supplements / vitamins | FDA DSHEA; cGMP facility; structure/function claims only; label requirements |
| Cosmetics (serums, lotions, lip, patches) | FDA MoCRA facility & product registration (in effect 2025–2026); ingredient restrictions; no drug claims |
| Electronics (massagers, meat grinders, power banks, lights) | FCC certification; UL/ETL safety; CPSC for batteries; UN38.3 for lithium battery transport |
| Pet products | AAFCO/FDA for pet food & supplements; general product safety; avoid medical claims |
| Apparel / textiles | FTC textile fiber labeling; CPSIA for children's items; CA Prop 65 awareness |
| All imports | US tariff policy on China-origin goods changed materially in 2025 (de minimis exemption ended); confirm current Section 301 rates and duty rules with your freight broker before pricing |
