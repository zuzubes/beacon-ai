EdTech is experiencing significant growth in both China and the US, evidenced by the 707 deals in these markets. Despite the relatively lower velocity of growth and deal volume (0.30), the sector is maturing, indicating potential for innovation and disruption.

With a strong still-early signal in India (0.85), the EdTech sector presents an opportunity due to its unsaturated nature. Current deal count stands at 95, suggesting a conducive environment for new entrants and solutions that cater to local needs.

### Key risks
- Lower sector size potential (0.23) compared to other categories
- Moderate transferability score (0.45) may indicate challenges in replicating successful models from abroad

### Confidence note
